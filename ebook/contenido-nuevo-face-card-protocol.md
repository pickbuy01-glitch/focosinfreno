## 1. Face yoga: los 7 ejercicios que faltan (06–12)

**Dónde va:** a continuación de *Forehead Resistance*, en una página nueva justo después de la página de face yoga. Así el ebook muestra los 12 que promete.

**Título de la página:**
# THE OTHER SEVEN.
# SAME 10 MINUTES.
## *NO SKIPPING.*

Five got you started. These seven finish the job: jaw, eyes, smile lines, neck, lips and the under-chin. Do them in order. Breathe through your nose the whole time.

---

**06 · THE JAWLINE SNATCH**
`10 REPS · 10 SEC · JAWLINE`
Tilt your chin slightly up. Push your lower jaw forward until your bottom teeth sit in front of your top teeth. Lift your lower lip up and hold. Feel it pull from your chin all the way to your ears.
*If your jaw clicks or hurts, stop. This one should burn, never ache.*

**07 · THE V**
`6 REPS · 5 SEC · EYES & UNDER-EYE`
Place your middle fingers between your brows and your index fingers at the outer corners of your eyes, like a V. Look up at the ceiling and squint your lower lids up, hard. Release slowly.
*Your eyelids should flutter by rep 4. That's the muscle waking up.*

**08 · THE AIR SWISH**
`30 SEC · 3 ROUNDS · SMILE LINES`
Fill your mouth with air. Move it: right cheek, left cheek, upper lip, lower lip. Like mouthwash, but make it skincare.
*Lowkey the best one for the lines that run from your nose to your mouth.*

**09 · THE GIRAFFE**
`4 REPS · 5 SEC · NECK`
Fingertips on your collarbones, pressing the skin gently down. Tilt your head all the way back and look at the ceiling. Push your lower lip out as far as it goes. Hold. Come back to center slowly.
*Your neck is part of your face card. Most people forget it exists.*

**10 · THE POUT PULSE**
`20 PULSES · 2 SETS · LIPS & MOUTH AREA`
Press your lips together and push them forward into a tight pout. Pulse forward and release, small and fast. Keep your cheeks relaxed. Only the lips work.
*The tiny lines above your lip? This is the move for them.*

**11 · THE TONGUE PRESS**
`10 REPS · 5 SEC · UNDER-CHIN`
Close your mouth and press your WHOLE tongue hard against the roof of your mouth. Hold while you tilt your head slightly up. Release. Repeat.
*It's mewing on max volume. Feel the tension under your chin = you're doing it right.*

**12 · THE LION**
`3 REPS · 5 SEC · FULL FACE RELEASE`
Inhale through your nose. Open your mouth as wide as it goes, stick your tongue out toward your chin, eyes wide and looking up. Exhale hard through your mouth. Relax everything.
*Always end here. You just worked 50+ muscles. Let them reset.*

> **Your 10-minute order:** Sky Kiss → Cheek Lift → Fish Face → Jawline Snatch → The V → Air Swish → Pout Pulse → Exaggerated Vowels → Forehead Resistance → Giraffe → Tongue Press → Lion.

### RETINOL RULES · READ BEFORE NIGHT ONE

- **Start low, go slow.** 0.025% to 0.05%, 2 nights a week. Add a night only when your skin stops complaining.
- **Pea-sized. For your whole face.** More is not better. More is just more irritation.
- **Skip the danger zones:** eyelids, corners of your nose and corners of your mouth.
- **Sensitive skin?** Sandwich it: thin layer of moisturizer → retinol → moisturizer.
- **Don't mix the same night** with AHAs, BHAs (glycolic, salicylic) or benzoyl peroxide.
- **The purge is real.** Some breakouts or flaking in weeks 2–6 can be normal. Burning, swelling or a rash is not. Stop.
- **Pregnant or breastfeeding?** No retinol. Swap it for bakuchiol, after your doctor says yes.
- **No SPF the next morning = no point.** Retinol makes your skin more sun-sensitive.

### 4A. Cold therapy & lymphatic drainage at home

**Dónde va:** Fase 1, después de *The Anti-Puff Diet*. El índice lo promete ("Cold therapy and lymphatic drainage at home") y el plan lo pide ("Cold gua sha", "Daily cold therapy").

# COLD IS YOUR
# *FASTEST* DE-PUFF.

Cold makes your blood vessels contract. Less fluid sitting in your face, less puffiness, in minutes. It's temporary, which is why you do it every morning.

**Pick one. 2 minutes max.**

**THE ICE ROLLER**
`1–2 MIN · EVERY MORNING`
Keep it in the freezer. Roll from the center of your face outward, then down the neck. Same direction as your gua sha.
*Easiest one to keep up with. Start here.*

**COLD GUA SHA**
`7 MIN · DAYS 1–7`
Put your gua sha in the fridge overnight, not the freezer. Then follow the 6-step protocol exactly as written.
*Two de-puff tools in one. Efficient.*

**THE FACE DUNK**
`3 DIPS · 10 SEC EACH`
A bowl of cold water with a few ice cubes. Hold your breath, dip your face for 10 seconds, come up, repeat 3 times.
*Model trick. Wakes you up faster than coffee.*

**THE SPOON TRICK**
`2 SPOONS · 5 MIN IN THE FREEZER`
Place the backs of two cold spoons under your eyes. Hold for 30 seconds, then glide them outward to your temples.
*Big night? Bad sleep? This is your 2-minute fix.*

> **Never put ice directly on your skin.** Wrap it in a thin cloth. Max 2 minutes per area. Skip cold if you have rosacea or cold sensitivity.

---

**NO TOOL? USE YOUR HANDS.**
`3 MIN · LYMPHATIC DRAINAGE`

Light pressure, like you're moving lotion, not muscle. Always toward your collarbones.

1. **Open the drain.** Gently press the hollow above your collarbones, 10 times.
2. **Neck.** Flat hands, stroke from below your ears down to your collarbones, 10 times.
3. **Jaw.** From your chin along the jaw to your ears, then down the neck, 5 times.
4. **Cheeks.** From the sides of your nose out to your ears, then down the neck, 5 times.
5. **Under-eye.** Ring finger only. Inner corner to temple, then down the neck. 3 times. Barely any pressure.
6. **Forehead.** Center out to the temples, then down the sides of your face to the neck, 5 times.

*Same rule as gua sha: always end at the collarbones. That's where it drains.*

### 4B. Sleep: the de-puff happens at night

**Dónde va:** Fase 1, junto a la página de cold therapy o al lado de la tarjeta "Bad sleep". La tarjeta nombra el sueño como causa de la hinchazón, pero el ebook no da consejos para dormir mejor.

# YOUR FACE IS BUILT
# *AT NIGHT.*

Bad sleep raises cortisol, and cortisol holds on to water. The puffy face you wake up with was decided the night before.

**THE NIGHT-BEFORE CHECKLIST**

☐ **7–9 hours.** Non-negotiable.
☐ **Sleep on your back.** Side sleeping presses your face into the pillow for 8 hours. Fluid pools where it's pressed.
☐ **One extra pillow.** Slightly elevated head = fluid drains instead of settling in your face.
☐ **Silk or satin pillowcase.** Less friction on your skin and hair than cotton.
☐ **No salty food 3 hours before bed.** Late-night chips show up at 8 a.m.
☐ **Last drink of alcohol 3+ hours before bed.** Or skip it. Your face will thank you.
☐ **Big glass of water earlier, small sips later.** Hydrate during the day, not right before bed.
☐ **Screens down 30 minutes before.** Better sleep, less cortisol.

*Can't sleep on your back yet? Start with the extra pillow. One change at a time.*

### 4C. Lemon water: the honest version

**Dónde va:** como recuadro pequeño en la página del *Anti-Puff Diet* o en la del plan de 30 días ("Lemon water first thing in the morning").

**WHY LEMON WATER?**
Real talk: the lemon isn't magic. The water is. After 8 hours of sleep you wake up dehydrated, and a dehydrated body holds on to water. A big glass first thing in the morning tells it to let go. The lemon just makes it a ritual you'll actually keep.

**How:** 1 big glass of room-temperature water + juice of ½ lemon. Drink it before coffee.
*Drink it with a straw or rinse your mouth after. Lemon is acidic, and your teeth matter too.*
